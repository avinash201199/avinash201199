I'm a software developer, builder, and community creator focused on building useful products for developers, students, job seekers, and founders.

I started by building small projects, experimenting with different technologies, and sharing resources with other developers. Over time, that evolved into something much bigger → building products and communities that help people discover opportunities, learn faster, and build better careers.

Today, most of my time goes into:

## What I'm Building

### Let's Code

**Let's Code** is a technology and career platform built for developers and students.

The goal is simple:

> Make it easier for people in tech to discover opportunities, learn the right skills, and become job-ready.

The platform brings together career resources, jobs, internships, interview preparation, developer resources, and AI-powered tools.

Some of the things I've been building:

* Job and internship discovery
* Job Finder
* Job Tracker
* Job Ready Score
* AI Resume Studio
* LinkedIn Optimizer
* Cover Letter AI
* Mock Interview tools
* Personalized Study Plan Generator
* Interview preparation resources
* Developer resource kits
* Company and hiring resources
* AI and developer tools

The platform is continuously evolving based on feedback from the community.

→ **Website:** https://www.lets-code.co.in/

## StartupsMap

**StartupsMap** is a startup discovery platform I'm building to make it easier to discover startups and what they're building.

There are thousands of interesting startups being built, but many of them never get enough visibility.

StartupsMap is built around a simple idea:

> Great startups should be easier to discover.

The platform is focused on helping people:

→ Discover startups
→ Explore what founders are building
→ Find interesting products
→ Discover companies and opportunities
→ Give early-stage startups more visibility

I'm building it as a growing directory and discovery platform for the startup ecosystem.

→ **Explore StartupsMap:** https://startupsmap.co.in/

If you're building a startup, you can add it to the platform and get discovered by people in the tech community.

# My Approach to Building

I enjoy working at the intersection of **technology, products, distribution, and community**.

For me, building isn't only about writing code.

It's about understanding a problem → building a solution → getting it in front of people → collecting feedback → improving it.

A typical project can go through:

**Problem → Idea → Build → Launch → Distribution → Feedback → Iterate**

This is the part of building that interests me the most.

I enjoy taking an idea from a simple thought or conversation and turning it into something people can actually use.
